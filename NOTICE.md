# Parallel: credits and notices

Parallel is a modified version of **Sidro** by Hitakumori
(<https://github.com/Hitakumori/Sidro>), used under the MIT License. The
original copyright notice and license text are kept in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## What Parallel adds

- My Items & Sets, Mastery List and Arcanes tabs (`set_catalog.py`, `arcane_catalog.py`).
- Rank-aware pricing and listing for arcanes.
- Extra selling presets (Most Common -2, Lowest -1p).
- A new look with selectable themes and accent colours.
